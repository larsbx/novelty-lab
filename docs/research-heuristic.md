# Non-associativity as a finite boundary

The useful part of the heuristic is retained:

- do not smooth associators away;
- encode them as exact finite transformations;
- organize transformations into orbit and holonomy strata;
- count only after stabilizers and invariance are certified.

The original biconditional “closure is achieved exactly when defect
stratification predicts all occupancies” is retired. It mixed an algebraic
property (closure), a representation choice (normalized left
multiplication), and a counting theorem (Burnside).

The replacement program treats parenthesizations as vertices of a finite
rebracketing graph. Elementary rotations are labelled by algebraic defect
transports. Flat cycles correspond to path-independent rebracketing on the
chosen state; nontrivial holonomy records the discrete boundary created by
non-associativity. The construction uses only finite-field multiplication,
inversion of nonzero-norm elements, matrices, group actions, and integer
counts.
