## 3. Terminology

A **guarded spread** is a rational invariant of non-null projective rays. **Semilinear covariance** means applying the associated field automorphism to the spread value, not unconditional equality. **Tensor direction rank**, **CW cell dimension**, **number of influence labels** and **materialization budget** are separate. A **certified swap** means exact equality of state-dependent endpoint vectors at a common prefix **and** a declared geometric pass. A **comparison certificate** records an unequal endpoint defect without making it a true commutation. **Invariant class coverage** means exclusion from the image of the inclusion-induced homology map; deleting one witness representative is insufficient.

## 4. Hypotheses and transfer limits

- The repository already has bounded exact F5 P3–P6 studies and pinned domain-neutral Mojo kernels. They are not actual LLM training audits; their negative controls show geometric positivity with noncommuting updates and nonzero isotropic residuals.
- Proof-by-finite-enumeration does not establish a formal polynomial identity over all fields. No novelty follows from reusing known quadratic, Cayley, subgroup, permutohedral or homology constructions.
- Real/float training execution, optimizer nondeterminism and probabilistic route semantics require separately specified operational models and proof of faithful abstraction.
- A finite field has no compatible order; any spread threshold is a declared field-value membership gate, with Frobenius stability separately required if semilinear invariance is claimed.
- Aschbacher subgroup classes inform possible stabilizers but do not prove maximality for arbitrary tensor packages.

## 5. Negative controls

1. Triple spreads of three pairwise orthogonal non-null vectors are \((1,1,1)\), refuting unrestricted planar triple-spread claims.
2. Same-sign Zorn cross-product multiplication fails norm composition under the circulated convention.
3. Nonzero null swap residuals must fail whole-vector equality.
4. Spread gate passing does not imply update operators commute or that output distributions are unchanged.
5. A single Gram determinant ratio does not fully classify pairs of higher-rank planes.
6. A witness-hit constraint does not necessarily remove every representative of a homology class.
7. A quantized or finite-field projected ML trace cannot by itself establish properties of the original floating execution.
