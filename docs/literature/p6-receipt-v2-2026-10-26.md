**Decision:** proceed, narrowed to an externally replayable **exact finite F5 experiment**. The existing `rt-route-stream/v1` strictly supports three to five influences. Do not retrofit a six-event receipt into v1 or assert a theorem about real neural-network training.

## Proposed specimen

Add a new, versioned `rt-route-stream/v2-p6` receipt envelope over **six** explicit F5^8 affine update definitions. Reuse the existing single-source exact step interpreter, projective and rank-two RT adapter, and P6 on-demand face-incidence driver. Encode all 1800 adjacent-swap witnesses or a canonical requested-only subset, and independently replay every witness against a separately supplied trusted SHA-256 specification pin. Permit higher coherence cell admissions only after replay and recursively verified facet closure. The external SHA-256 is a trusted *pin* when supplied from an independent channel, not a signature.

## Hypotheses and negative controls

- Fixed field F5, six specified non-null update offsets, explicit eight-coordinate update vectors, exact integer F5 arithmetic, and a **declared v2** affine-update interpreter. The program specification pins the *parameters and operational language version*, not an independently verified interpreter binary.
- A receipt is canonical sorted-key, compact ASCII JSON with one terminating LF. The digest includes that LF; duplicate keys are rejected by canonical re-encoding.
- Local swap equality is a whole-vector equality at the *common actual prefix*, not a zero quadratic norm and not an equality after an arbitrary noninjective suffix. A geometric pass remains a distinct boolean.
- A forged residual with a refreshed receipt digest fails replay; a changed program with a refreshed own digest fails against the independently trusted original pin; a request containing incomplete/unordered/duplicate edges fails; wrong n, malformed field or rank, and unexpected schema keys fail closed.
- Partial receipts may certify one requested P6 square if all four edges are present but **cannot** certify P6 top coherence. An out-of-budget face request must not leave partially constructed cache state. The v1 program continues to reject six influences.
- No signature/authorship guarantee, no sound floating-point reduction, no statistical learning, no full arbitrary-rank P_R, no probabilistic audit property, and no model training run is established.

## Decision

**Proceed with narrowed experimental v2** as additions under `experiments/rt/`, `schemas/`, `tests/`, and `docs/experiments/`. No edits to vendor, shared finite-field kernels, proof ledger, or generated sources. A full repository CI success is required before merge.
