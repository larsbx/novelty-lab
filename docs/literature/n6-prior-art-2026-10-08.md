## Equivalent formulations and terminology

For finite connected simple $\Gamma$ with an edge, these tests describe the
**same ordinary-embedding property**:

| Formulation | Exact content and source |
|---|---|
| Fiber containment / S-prime | Every embedding in a binary Cartesian product has a constant coordinate. S5 §1 defines “nontrivial” by both projections having at least two vertices. This is stronger than “$\Gamma$ is isomorphic to a subgraph of one factor somewhere.” |
| Complete-factor witness | It suffices to search for a mixed embedding into $K_a\square K_b$, $a,b\ge2$: enlarge each finite projection to a complete graph. S6 Lemma 2.1. This is an ordinary, not induced, embedding reduction. |
| Path-color obstruction | S-composite iff there is a surjective path-$k$-coloring, $2\le k\le \lvert V\Gamma\rvert-1$. A path with differently colored consecutive vertices must have differently colored endpoints. It need not be a proper coloring. S5 Theorem 1; S7 Theorem 1.1. |
| Edge-label obstruction | S-composite iff a surjective two-labeling of edges has at least three cyclic label changes on every induced cycle using both labels (necessarily at least four). S6 Theorem 2.2 / Corollary 2.3. The cycle condition is essential. |
| Quotient-coordinate witness | Partition $E\Gamma=F_1\sqcup F_2$. Coordinates are components of $\Gamma\setminus F_i$; S6 §2 proves the quotient map injective under its labeling condition. Checking both coordinate variation and injectivity yields a replayable negative witness. |
| Count defect | $N_\Gamma(G\square H)-\varepsilon(H)N_\Gamma(G)-\varepsilon(G)N_\Gamma(H)$ counts mixed copies. It vanishes universally iff S-prime. This is the audit's elementary comparison inference, not a theorem promoted here. |

The last row works because a connected nontrivial copy in a fiber has a unique
fiber direction, so the two fiber collections are disjoint. It fails for
$K_1$, which belongs to both collections. In positive characteristic,
nonzero mixed counts can disappear modulo the characteristic.

Terminology recommendations:

- First mention: **fiber-captured (Cartesian S-prime)**; use S-prime in
  literature comparisons and cite S5–S7. Historical **quasiprime** is a
  search synonym, not a newly coined property.
- **Cartesian-prime** concerns factorization of the entire graph. It does
  not forbid mixed embeddings as a proper subgraph.
- **Fiber-complemented** in S8 concerns gated sets, gate maps and prefibers.
  Its accessible definition is metric/projection-based, unlike universal
  ordinary pattern embeddings. Full-paper access remains unresolved; no
  absence claim about its uninspected theorems is needed.
- **Quasi Cartesian products**, Cartesian skeletons, bundles, convexity,
  retracts and partial cubes concern different relations or embedding
  restrictions. Shared words do not identify S-primeness.

## Classical calculus and the remaining counting boundary

Write $A=\mathbb Z[x_P:P\text{ connected Cartesian-prime}]$ and
$\varepsilon(x_P)=|V(P)|$. From S10,
$\Omega_{A/\mathbb Z}=\bigoplus_P A\,dx_P$. Thus a derivation into
$\mathbb Z_\varepsilon$ is specified by arbitrary integers $a_P$, with
$D(f)=\sum_P a_P\,(\partial f/\partial x_P)(\varepsilon)$.
Each sum is finite, while the space of choices is a **direct product**.
The same observation holds for any fixed character. This is standard
commutative algebra applied to S1, not a separate graph-theoretic theorem.

For comparison only, define
$\delta_gF(x)=F(gx)-\varepsilon(g)F(x)$. S10's operator order is tested by
iterated such commutators. An order-one operator can include a scalar
$\varepsilon$ term; a derivation also has $F(1)=0$.
A downstream “principal symbol” must say whether the iterated defect
evaluated at 1 is divided by a factorial.

For rooted closed walks $W_m(G)=\operatorname{tr}(A_G^m)$, $W_0=\varepsilon$
and $W_1=0$. S9's two identities imply

$$
W_m(G\square H)=\sum_{j=0}^m\binom mj W_j(G)W_{m-j}(H).
$$

**Comparison inference:** in a $k$-fold top defect of $W_{2k}$, every
occupied factor needs at least two steps. Only the all-two-step term survives
at the top, and $W_2=2|E|$ gives $(2k)!\,|E|^{\otimes k}$ with the
undivided convention. $K_2$ factors make it nonzero. This explains why the
proposed even-walk symbol is a routine consequence; it is not a proof record
or originality finding. Triangle-free and bipartite domains differ:
$W_5(C_5)=10$ and $W_7(C_7)=14$.

By contrast, ordinary $c_m=\operatorname{inj}(C_m,-)/(2m)$ removes all
noninjective walks, while induced cycles remove chords as well. S12 uses
induced counts and a coloring-graph/Hamming setting. Neither its formulas nor
walk traces can replace the unspecified general $c_6$ theorem.
S11 is a particularly relevant precedent: its induced-cube polynomial
multiplies under Cartesian product and it defines derivation graphs of
median graphs. Compare the actual structures before describing the entire
graph calculus as unprecedented.

## Stop/go conclusions and next evidence needed

The overall decision is **redirect** on positive prior art, not on an empty
search result. Strongest threats: S1 for the ring, S5–S6 for fiber-capture,
S7 for recognition, S9–S10 for walk-symbol repackaging, and S11/S13–S17 for
broad “first graph calculus” or unification language.

Concrete next tasks in the standalone repository:

1. Revise attribution, the $K_1$ exception and obsolete open problems.
2. Supply the complete $c_6$ formula, normalizations, hypotheses, and the
   precise triangle-free statement. Compare ordinary/induced cycle and
   hypercube specializations source by source. A symbol matching one cube is
   a control, not universal clearance.
3. Gate an explicitly restricted S-prime algorithm or parameter/certificate
   bound; cite existing path/edge-label constructions and complexity.
4. For an algebraic bridge, specify source/target objects, addition,
   multiplication, unit, coproduct and differential filtration. Mismatched
   multiplication already blocks an unqualified equivalence.

No new theorem, new open problem in the field, or novelty status is
established here. Missing full texts and the absent exact manuscript are
listed limitations, not hidden evidence of originality. All proof, experiment,
formalization and manuscript authority stays outside `novelty-lab`;
Vizing-Domination issue #10 remains a future consumer relationship.
