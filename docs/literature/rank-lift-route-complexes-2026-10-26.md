# Rank-lifted RT route complexes — bounded literature gate (2026-10-26)

**Decision:** **proceed with a narrowed target** — a small, non-authoritative, exact finite-field experiment comparing *cubical additive* versus *permutohedral order* coherence, with a parameterized Grassmann direction adapter. No new theorem, model-training result, or novelty claim is promoted.

## 1. Proposed claim or experiment

Over the prime field `F_5`, reuse the existing independent split-quadratic RT oracle to evaluate guarded spreads for three non-null directions and a rank-2 Gram-determinant direction adapter. Build two finite combinatorial models for three audited influences: the 2-dimensional permutohedron `P_3` (hexagon with an optional 2-cell) and a 3-cube (six square faces with an optional 3-cell). Check incidence boundaries, ranks/homology, and rejection of insufficient gates. This experiments with **coherence shape** independently of **tensor direction rank**, and separates *audit legality* from *operational equality*.

## 3. Terminology

**Coherence rank** is the number of distinct influence labels in a local experiment, not CW cell dimension and not tensor order. **Tensor direction rank** is the dimension of a presented Grassmann subspace. **Audit rank** is the highest requested cell dimension. **Sparse materialization** means on-demand construction of relevant faces together with their boundaries; it does not restrict these mathematical ranks.

## 4. Transferrable and nontransferrable hypotheses

- Field `F_5`, characteristic odd, exact modular arithmetic, explicit non-null denominators; no ordered threshold or floating-point distance.
- The permutation branch models **permitted formal route rewrites**. A hexagon 2-cell records braid coherence only when separately authorized; a spread match alone is *not* proof of semantic equality for state-dependent training updates.
- The cubical branch models additions from a **frozen** set of contributions. The cube is not a general model of noncommutative update maps.
- A rank-2 Gram-ratio measures a property of two nondegenerate subspaces; it need not be a metric and it cannot be called a tensor-analytic principal-angle theorem over `F_5`.
- `P_3` is a 2-cell; a true 3-dimensional permutohedral experiment requires `P_4` and a separate full face/attaching-map implementation.

## 5. Negative controls

1. Remove the hexagon face: the permutation 1-skeleton retains a nontrivial first cycle.
2. Remove the cubical 3-cell: the six faces give a nonzero 2-homology class; restoring the 3-cell kills it.
3. Gate off spread=1 between orthogonal directions: no higher coherence face may be asserted despite the underlying combinatorial possibilities.
4. Null vector input: guarded spread refuses the operation.
5. Change bases within rank-2 subspaces: Gram-ratio is unchanged; use a distinct plane to avoid testing only identical spans.
6. Do not infer model-training equivalence or approximate error bounds from geometry alone.

## 6. Decision

**Proceed with a narrowed target.** Implement a deterministic, bounded, non-authoritative `experiments/rt/` driver and contract-labelled tests. Record results as experiment evidence only, without a ledger claim, generated data change, or mathematical novelty assertion. Verify combinatorial `∂²=0` and the negative controls. Stop before extrapolating to LLM training correctness or general permutohedral rank `R`.
