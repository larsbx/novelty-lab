# G₂-invariants of several octonions: stop/go note

Gate for: promoting paper Conjecture 3.32 (`WordDefectG2Data`) to a theorem,
and for H3 tests that condition on the φ- and ψ-data of the letters.

## 1. Proposed claim

Let ℓ be an odd prime, let C be the cayley-dickson/v1 algebra with
parameters (−1, −1, −1) over 𝔽_ℓ, and let a₁, …, aₙ ∈ C have nonzero norm.
Write p_i for the trace-zero parts, B for the polar form, φ(p, q, r) = B(pq, r)
and ψ(p, q, r, s) = B([p, q, r], s).

**Claim.** The characteristic polynomial of the word defect
W(a₁, …, aₙ) = L_{a₁}⋯L_{aₙ}L_v⁻¹ is a function of three kinds of data:
- the Gram matrix of (1, a₁, …, aₙ);
- the values of φ on triples of the p_i;
- the values of ψ on quadruples of the p_i.

The same holds in characteristic 0.

## 2. Sources

Read on 2026-10-05.

1. **A. N. Zubkov and I. P. Shestakov, *Invariants of G₂ and Spin(7) in positive
   characteristic*.** arXiv:1512.06354v2 (11 Mar 2016); Transformation Groups,
   doi:10.1007/s00031-017-9435-8. These results are used:
   - **Prop. 3.2:** for char F ≠ 2, every Λᵏ(O₀) is a tilting G₂-module.
   - **Prop. 3.6:** the Hilbert–Poincaré series of R(n) = F[O₀ⁿ]^{G₂} does
     not depend on char F ≠ 2.
   - **Intro, p. 1:** F[Oⁿ]^{G₂} ≅ R(n) ⊗ F[t(z₁), …, t(zₙ)].
   - **Cor. 7.11:** R(n) is generated by
     - t(x_i x_j);
     - t((x_i x_j)x_k);
     - Q′(ijkl), the complete skew-symmetrization of t(((x_i x_j)x_k)x_l).
   - **Remark 7.12:** Thm. 7.10 and Cor. 7.11 hold over any infinite field of
     odd characteristic.
   - **Proof of Cor. 7.11:** t(12)t(34), t(13)t(24), t(14)t(23) and Q′(1234)
     are linearly independent in every odd characteristic, including 3.
2. **G. W. Schwarz, *Invariant theory of G₂ and Spin₇*, Comment. Math. Helv. 63
   (1988) 624–663.** This is the characteristic-0 first fundamental theorem with
   the same generators. I have *not* read it; the statement used here is Zubkov
   and Shestakov's account of it (Introduction; "[24]").
3. **R. Howe, *The first fundamental theorem of invariant theory and spherical
   subgroups*, Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 56 (1994) 333–346.** This is a second
   proof in characteristic 0, cited by source 1 and not read.
4. **C. de Concini and C. Procesi, *A characteristic free approach to invariant
   theory*, Adv. Math. 21 (1976) 330–354.** O(n)-invariants of several vectors
   are generated by the inner products, in every characteristic ≠ 2. This
   is the classical baseline, and it is what "classical data" means in the paper.
5. **T. A. Springer and F. D. Veldkamp, *Octonions, Jordan algebras and
   exceptional groups*, Ch. 1.** Over an algebraically closed field every
   octonion algebra is split, so it is isomorphic to Zorn's vector-matrix
   algebra, the model of source 1.

## 3. Terminology

- **Vector invariants / first fundamental theorem (FFT):** generators of
  F[Vⁿ]^G.
- **Spinor invariants:** source 1's route through Spin(7).
- **Generic octonions z_i, traceless parts x_i:** the polynomial-ring
  formulation of the invariant ring.
- **The associative calibration φ and coassociative form ψ:** the
  differential-geometric names for these forms.

## 4. Hypotheses

**Transfer:**
- Odd characteristic: ℓ is odd.
- Infinite field: pass to 𝔽̄_ℓ. W is equivariant under Aut(C ⊗ 𝔽̄_ℓ)
  (paper Proposition 3.31; the proof is algebraic), and G₂(𝔽̄_ℓ) is dense in G₂.
- Choice of model: over 𝔽̄_ℓ our algebra is isomorphic to Zorn's algebra
  (source 5). Traces, norms and products correspond under the isomorphism.
- Translation of the generators:
  - for trace-zero p and q, t(pq) = −B(p, q);
  - t((pq)r) = −φ(p, q, r);
  - Q′ is an alternating G₂-invariant 4-form on O₀.
- Q′ in terms of ψ:
  - By source 1, Prop. 3.2, Λ⁴(O₀*) is tilting, so it has a good filtration.
    The space of G₂-invariant alternating 4-forms therefore has its
    characteristic-0 dimension, which is 1.
  - ψ is a nonzero element of that space for every odd ℓ. Sampled integer
    values of ψ in our model have gcd 4, so they share no odd prime factor
    (`tests/test_word_g2.py`).
  - Hence Q′ = c·ψ with c ∈ 𝔽̄_ℓ^×.
  - This argument avoids normalization. The unnormalized skew-sum equals 12ψ
    over ℤ, which vanishes mod 3, yet Q′ itself does not vanish mod 3 (source
    1, proof of Cor. 7.11).
- Polynomiality: the coefficients of the characteristic polynomial of W are
  c_k(M)/N(v)^k. Here M = L_{a₁}⋯L_{aₙ}L_{v̄} has polynomial entries, and
  N(v) = ∏ N(a_i) is Gram data.

**Do not transfer:**
- Characteristic 2. It is excluded, as everywhere in the paper.
- The step from "the generators separate the values of invariant polynomials"
  to "the G₂-orbits are separated". The FFT gives the first only. The claim
  needs nothing more, but any orbit statement would need it.
- Schwarz's characteristic-0 statement, as quoted rather than read. Until it
  is read, the characteristic-0 half of the claim rests on source 1's account
  of it.

## 5. Negative controls

- **φ alone and ψ alone:** `data/n2/word-g2-v1.json` stores a certificate for
  each in which preserving that form, but not the other, changes the
  characteristic polynomial. Both forms are therefore needed. That fits the
  FFT, in which t((x_i x_j)x_k) and Q′ are independent generators.
- **Length 3:** paper Proposition 3.26 shows that for three letters the
  characteristic polynomial does not depend on φ (characteristic 0 or > 8).
  The FFT permits a dependence there, and Prop. 3.26 shows there is none.
- **Characteristic 3:** the unnormalized skew-sum vanishes. The argument
  above routes around it.

## 6. Decision

**Proceed, narrowed.**
- The claim holds for every odd ℓ and in characteristic 0. It is a direct
  consequence of the FFT for G₂ (Schwarz; Zubkov–Shestakov) together with
  Proposition 3.31.
- It becomes paper Theorem 3.32, resting on the imported record
  `G2FirstFundamentalTheorem`. The census `WordDefectG2Data` stays as
  corroborating evidence.
- **Novelty:** none for the invariant theory. What the repository adds is the
  observation that word-defect characteristic polynomials are G₂-invariant,
  and the certificates showing that both φ and ψ are needed.
- **For H3:** the characteristic polynomial of a word defect carries no
  information beyond the B-, φ- and ψ-data of its letters. Any
  defect-stratified collision signal is therefore G₂ bookkeeping, and the
  integral-shell enrichment (`H3IntegralPilot`) has to be read that way.
