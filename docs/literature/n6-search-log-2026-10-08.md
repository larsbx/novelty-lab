## Stopping boundary and conservative negative findings

The gate stops after coverage of all five requested clusters, positive
identification of the closest S-prime/complexity/ring sources, and inspection
of concrete calculus/counting neighbors. It does **not** stop because an
arbitrary number of no-hit queries would prove novelty.

The decisive stop conclusions are positive matches: S5/S6 for the property
and characterization, S7 for recognition, and S1/S10 for abstract algebra.
The walk-symbol assessment is explicitly a standard-consequence comparison
from S9/S10. No exact general ordinary-cycle theorem or Cartesian-compatible
Hopf bridge was established in the inspected passages; this is a bounded
finding, not an assertion of absence. Hypercube-count references found in
the last cycle follow-up are leads, not clearance.

Remaining limits are the unavailable complete manuscript/formulas,
full Chastand/Joyal/Schmitt and some book/factorization access, and a
nonexhaustive cycle/Gray-code/hypercube literature search. Before any remaining
theorem candidate is promoted, the standalone repository must state it
exactly and commission a targeted follow-up with those conventions.
`novelty-lab` records this gate and does not create proof, experiment or
manuscript authority.
