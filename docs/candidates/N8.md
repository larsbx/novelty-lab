# N8 — Scoped deontic evidence arithmetic

**Status:** candidate

Use a finite synthetic policy model for a request scoped by actor, action,
resource and policy context. Each evidence item has a stable identity,
polarity, scope and applicability conditions. Inputs to a merge for one
request are pairs \((P,F)\) of applicable permission and prohibition
evidence. Merge componentwise by union, and retain the source identities.
The four outcomes are neither, permission only, prohibition only, and
conflict. Evaluate applicability against the same fixed snapshot before
merging. Treat incompatible payloads for the same evidence identity as
an explicit input error.

Merge and resolution are separate operations. For the first fixture,
admission requires permission evidence and an empty prohibition set.
A general priority policy would be a separate, versioned rule whose
decision retains the grounds on both sides. A separate duty ledger keys
obligations by duty identity, with deadline and discharge conditions.
Repeated delivery of a duty deduplicates by identity. Two independent
duties can coexist even when their descriptions are identical.

Use a synthetic release grant and an active publication hold. Check all
four evidence states, duplicated deliveries, different actors and
resources, stale grants, and reordered merge inputs. The conflict must
remain visible and yield no admission in the initial fixture. A
cross-scope grant and a replay after the applicability interval ends are
adversarial controls. A second fixture should distinguish replay of one
duty from two independently created duties.

Compare with algebraic deontic action logic, four-valued evidence
semantics and policy combination rules before implementing the model.
The proposed contribution is a replayable scoped-evidence contract
for a synthetic action workflow. Ḥukm and the deontic circuits remain
the consumers for domain policy and normative interpretations.
The [deontic action logic paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1570868309000378)
and [four-valued deontic semantics paper](https://philarchive.org/archive/KULDTR)
are initial literature locators.

After the individual contracts are checked, compose the publication
fixture with N10 for possible effects and N11 for replay of the admission
decision. Include a trace with an enabled action and a conflicting
admission decision to separate the two layers.
