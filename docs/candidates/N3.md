# N3 — Shell collision statistics modulo ℓ

**Status:** candidate — Poisson law is a heuristic.

For a fixed Hurwitz-order convention, push the norm-(n) shell to a precisely
defined finite quotient. The sample-space denominator, projectivization, unit
action, and inclusion of zero-occupancy fibers must be explicit.

## Baseline experiment

The checked-in analyzer takes the complete occupancy vector and reports:

- sample size, number of bins, empirical and expected mean;
- variance and index of dispersion;
- Pearson statistic with adjacent expected-count bins pooled until adequate;
- total variation distance from the fitted finite Poisson law, with the tail
  folded into the last observed class;
- a deterministic SHA-256 digest of the canonical input.

This is descriptive evidence only. A fitted Poisson curve is not an
independence proof, and the same data cannot both choose orbit corrections and
validate them.

## Required next data generator

Enumerate Hurwitz elements exactly, canonicalize the chosen unit action, reduce
through a documented quaternion-to-matrix map, and emit the entire finite
target occupancy vector. Test small shells against the theta coefficient
(24sigma_1(n)) under the selected convention.

## Theorem route

Express factorial moments as congruence-constrained representation counts.
Identify the precise level-(ell^2) theta series and split Eisenstein and cusp
parts. Do not cite “exact formulas” until the relevant space, character, and
uniformity in the collision regime are established.
