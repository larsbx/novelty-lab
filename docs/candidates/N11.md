# N11 — Composable arithmetic certificates

**Status:** candidate

Represent a certified step by a typed source, typed target, context,
rule identifier and replayable witness. Compose admitted steps by
concatenating their traces when their boundary states and contexts agree.
Check each witness and boundary independently under the declared rule
set. Compare associativity at the level of flat traces, with the empty
trace serving as an identity at its declared state.

Start from the three-state workflow in N10. Bind each step to a
versioned finite transition table and record its intermediate state.
Replay the two-step trace from its declared initial state and compare
the computed target. Mutate one intermediate state, a rule ID and a
context version in separate controls. Recomputing an artifact digest
after tampering must leave invalid semantics invalid. When integrating
with N8, also bind the replay to the request and the policy snapshot
used for admission.

Compare existing proof-record identities, finite witness replay and
compositional proof objects before choosing an artifact format.
Reuse the pinned proof-record infrastructure where its contract fits.
The possible contribution is a typed composition and checking contract
for these consumer witnesses. An artifact digest identifies content.
Checker acceptance rests on replay against the declared inputs and rules.
