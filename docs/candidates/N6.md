# N6 — Cartesian graph-product calculus and fiber-capture

**Status:** provisional candidate — literature/novelty gate only; standalone domain repository pending.

## Research object

Study finite graphs under disjoint union and Cartesian product through the ring

[
K_0^{\square}(\mathsf{FinGraph}),
]

with special emphasis on:

- the polynomial-ring description from unique Cartesian prime factorization;
- multiplicative graph characters;
- derivations and higher-order differential operators at the vertex-count character;
- **fiber-captured** connected graphs \(\Gamma\), for which every copy of \(\Gamma\) in every Cartesian product lies in a single fiber, equivalently the subgraph-count identity
  [
  N_\Gamma(G\square H)
  =N_\Gamma(G)|V(H)|+|V(G)|N_\Gamma(H)
  ]
  holds for all finite graphs \(G,H\);
- cycle and closed-walk order/principal-symbol formulas under Cartesian product.

This candidate is intentionally broader than one manuscript theorem. The novelty question concerns the **synthesis and new theorem package**, not classical Cartesian factorization, Kronecker-sum identities, or standard differential-operator formalism.

## Current mathematical boundary

The working manuscript history has already exposed several failure modes that the novelty audit must preserve:

- Cartesian-prime does **not** imply that the corresponding subgraph count is a derivation.
- Connected subgraphs of \(G\square H\) do **not** in general factor as products of their coordinate projections.
- Small-product numerical checks are not sufficient to certify fiber-capture of a large pattern.
- Computational agreement is evidence for a finite statement, not a novelty proof and not a general theorem.

The literature gate must therefore compare exact statements, not thematic resemblance.

## Candidate novelty questions

### N6-Q1 — Ring/calculus synthesis

Has the Grothendieck ring of finite graphs with addition by disjoint union and multiplication by Cartesian product been explicitly developed as a polynomial ring on Cartesian-prime connected graphs **together with** a differential calculus at natural graph characters?

Classical inputs such as Sabidussi factorization and polynomial/monoid-ring algebra are imported, not novelty claims.

### N6-Q2 — Fiber-capture

Does the exact condition

> every copy of a connected pattern \(\Gamma\) in every Cartesian product is contained in one fiber

already appear in equivalent form in Cartesian-product embedding, graph-bundle, convexity, retract, or recognition literature?

The audit must search for equivalent formulations even when the term *fiber-captured* is absent.

### N6-Q3 — Recognition and certificates

Is there prior work characterizing or deciding patterns that admit no mixed-factor embedding into Cartesian products?

Search neighboring literatures on:

- graph embeddings into Cartesian products;
- graph bundles;
- local/approximate Cartesian products;
- Cartesian skeletons and product recognition;
- retracts, gated subgraphs, convex subgraphs, and isometric embeddings when relevant;
- forbidden configurations and certificate complexity.

### N6-Q4 — Differential order of graph invariants

Are fixed cycle counts, closed-walk counts, or related graph-counting invariants studied by an operator-order filtration under Cartesian product, with principal symbols analogous to the manuscript's formulas?

Standard walk convolution from the Kronecker-sum identity is classical and must be separated from any new order/symbol theorem.

### N6-Q5 — Algebraic neighbors

How close are the following frameworks to the present construction?

- monoid algebras and graph products;
- graph algebras / quantum graphs / connection matrices;
- combinatorial species;
- incidence and graph Hopf algebras;
- universal Tutte characters;
- differential operators on commutative rings.

The comparison must state both overlap and missing structure; thematic similarity alone is not equivalence.

   - manuscript claim,
   - closest source,
   - exact overlap,
   - exact difference,

3. separate:
   - classical/imported facts,
   - new synthesis,

5. resolve terminology collision risk for **fiber-captured**;
6. identify the strongest prior-art threat to each headline theorem.

## Repository boundary

N6 is **not** the authority for the mathematical program.

The intended authority is a standalone research repository, tentatively:

- `cartesian-graph-calculus-research`, or
- `graph-product-ring-research`.

That repository should own:

- manuscript sources;
- proof/claim registry;
- exact verification package;
- fiber-capture experiments;
- cycle/walk computations;
- formalization.

`novelty-lab` owns only the bounded literature and novelty gate.

The Vizing-domination repository may consume proved graph-product lemmas through pinned imports or citations, but must not become authority for this theory.

## Stop conditions

Stop and revise rather than promote if:

- an equivalent fiber-capture characterization already exists under different terminology;
- an exact prior differential-order/principal-symbol formulation is found;
- a purported theorem relies only on finite product tests;
- a claim depends on an unproved numerical extrapolation;
- the manuscript conflates Cartesian primality with fiber-capture;
- classical product-factorization or spectral identities are presented as novel;
- the standalone repository does not distinguish theorem, computation, conjecture, and literature evidence.
