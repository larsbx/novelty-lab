# N2 — Octonionic navigation

**Status:** candidate — open questions, no lattice or expansion claim.

Let (mathcal C) denote a precisely selected integral octonion order with
(E_8) norm lattice. Before computation, fix whether generators are real
normalized maps, integral similitudes, triality triples, or reductions modulo
a prime. These are related but not interchangeable objects.

## Questions

1. Does the generated object admit an S-arithmetic interpretation and a
   natural action related to a (D_4) building?
2. For good primes (ell), what subgroup of the relevant finite orthogonal
   or spin group is generated?
3. What are the normalized nontrivial eigenvalues of the undirected,
   multiplicity-aware Cayley graph?
4. Is any covering exponent statement provable for the compact real group?

## Mandatory falsification checks

- Verify closure in the claimed group and compute determinants/forms exactly.
- Preserve duplicate generators and unit orbits until the quotient convention
  is explicit.
- Compare generated orders against the exact orders of candidate groups,
  including center/projective factors and plus/minus orthogonal type.
- Symmetrize the generating multiset or state that the operator is directed.
- Record primes excluded by bad reduction.

## Literature lanes

Search higher-rank golden gates, Ramanujan complexes, triality and integral
octonions, octonionic modular groups, Moufang/building actions, and spectral
results for finite orthogonal groups. Record exact queries and dates in a
future prior-art ledger.

## Finite defect census (`experiments/n2/defect_census.py`)

Model: C = A₃ of `cayley-dickson/v1` with parameters (−1, −1, −1) mod an odd
prime ℓ. The norm is a sum of eight squares, which is isotropic, so C is the
split octonion algebra. Δ(x, y) = L_x L_y L_{xy}⁻¹ is computed with
L_z⁻¹ = N(z)⁻¹ L_{z̄}, so no square root is taken. Per sampled pair the census
checks N2-T03 (isometry, fixed xy, kernel = vanishing associator), the
inverse formula, and three statements that go beyond N2-T03. Each of those
three is a pending record in the [ledger index](../ledger-index.md):

- `DefectFixesQuaternionSubalgebra`. Δ fixes Q = span{1, x, y, xy}
  pointwise. *Argument (unreviewed):* by Artin's theorem the subalgebra
  generated by x and y is associative and contains Q, so x(yz) = (xy)z for
  z ∈ Q. Since xy is invertible and Q is a subalgebra, L_{xy} maps Q onto Q.
  So every w ∈ Q is (xy)z with z ∈ Q, and Δw = x(yz) = w. As an isometry
  fixing Q, Δ preserves Q⊥ whenever Q is nondegenerate. The defect therefore
  lives in O(Q⊥) ≅ O(4), not merely in the SO(7) stabiliser of xy.
- `DefectSpecialOrthogonal`: det Δ = 1. The connectedness proof used over ℝ
  (N2-T01) does not transfer to 𝔽_ℓ.
- `DefectRankLaw`: rank(Δ − I) = 0, 2, 4 for dim Q ≤ 2, = 3, = 4. Coarse
  strata are therefore classical data (the second falsifier of NC-X-01, at
  rank level). Only the class of Δ restricted to Q⊥ can carry anything new.

The committed census `data/n2/defect-census-v1.json` (record `DefectCensus`,
an experimental result) covers ℓ ∈ {3, 5, 7, 11} with no violations. In it
dim Q = 3 is rare (6 of about 1,930 pairs), and dim Q = 1 appears only in the
unit tests. The open research question is `DefectStratifiedCollisions` (H3):
do collision moments of navigation words, conditioned on that class, carry
signal beyond ambient conjugacy data?
