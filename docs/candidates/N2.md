# N2 — Octonionic navigation

**Status:** candidate — open questions, no lattice or expansion claim.

Let (mathcal C) denote a precisely selected integral octonion order with
(E_8) norm lattice. Before computation, fix whether generators are real
normalized maps, integral similitudes, triality triples, or reductions modulo
a prime. These are related but not interchangeable objects.

## Questions

1. Does the generated object admit an S-arithmetic interpretation and a
   natural action related to a (D_4) building?
2. For good primes (ell), what subgroup of the relevant finite orthogonal
   or spin group is generated?
3. What are the normalized nontrivial eigenvalues of the undirected,
   multiplicity-aware Cayley graph?
4. Is any covering exponent statement provable for the compact real group?

## Mandatory falsification checks

- Verify closure in the claimed group and compute determinants/forms exactly.
- Preserve duplicate generators and unit orbits until the quotient convention
  is explicit.
- Compare generated orders against the exact orders of candidate groups,
  including center/projective factors and plus/minus orthogonal type.
- Symmetrize the generating multiset or state that the operator is directed.
- Record primes excluded by bad reduction.

## Literature lanes

Search higher-rank golden gates, Ramanujan complexes, triality and integral
octonions, octonionic modular groups, Moufang/building actions, and spectral
results for finite orthogonal groups. Record exact queries and dates in a
future prior-art ledger.

## Finite defect census (`experiments/n2/defect_census.py`)

Model: C = A₃ of `cayley-dickson/v1` with parameters (−1, −1, −1) mod an odd
prime ℓ. The norm is a sum of eight squares, which is isotropic, so C is the
split octonion algebra. Δ(x, y) = L_x L_y L_{xy}⁻¹ is computed with
L_z⁻¹ = N(z)⁻¹ L_{z̄}, so no square root is taken. Per sampled pair the census
checks N2-T03 (isometry, fixed xy, kernel = vanishing associator), the
inverse formula, and three statements that go beyond N2-T03, each a record
in the [ledger index](../ledger-index.md):

- `DefectFixesQuaternionSubalgebra` (**pending**,
  [written proof awaiting independent review](../theorems/defect-fixes-subalgebra.md)). Δ fixes pointwise the
  subalgebra A generated by x and y, which contains Q. It preserves W⊥ for
  every W ⊆ A, and rank(Δ − I) ≤ 8 − dim Q. When Q is nondegenerate, Δ is the
  identity on Q plus an element of O(Q⊥) ≅ O(4), not merely of the
  stabiliser of xy. The proof uses Artin's theorem and the composition
  identities, both imported with hypotheses checked.
- `DefectSpecialOrthogonal`: det Δ = 1. The connectedness proof used over ℝ
  (N2-T01) does not transfer to 𝔽_ℓ.
- `DefectRankLaw`: rank(Δ − I) = 0, 2, 4 for dim Q ≤ 2, = 3, = 4. Coarse
  strata are therefore classical data (the second falsifier of NC-X-01, at
  rank level).
- `DefectCharacteristicPolynomial` and `DefectClassIsClassical`: the
  characteristic polynomial of Δ is (X − 1)⁴ (X² − τX + 1)² with
  τ = 2 − det Gram_B(1, x, y) / (2 N(x) N(y)), and Δ is semisimple for
  τ ≠ ±2. Its similarity class is therefore classical data too (the same
  falsifier, at similarity level; claim KILL-09).
- `DefectReflectionFactorization` and `DefectSOClassFinite`: Δ = s_xv s_v
  s_yv s_(xy)v for v ∈ Q⊥, so its spinor norm is trivial, and over 𝔽_q a
  semisimple defect's SO(C, N)-class depends only on τ (claim KILL-10).
- Word defects W(a₁, …, aₙ) = L_{a₁} ⋯ L_{aₙ} L_v⁻¹, v the left-comb value:
  for n = 3 the characteristic polynomial is still classical
  (`WordDefectLengthThreeClassical`, a Clifford-trace argument), but for
  n = 4 it is not: a rotation of 1⊥ that preserves the Gram matrix of the
  letters changes it (`WordDefectNotClassical`, an explicit certificate over
  𝔽₃; `data/n2/word-defects-v1.json` finds the same for most random words of
  length 4 and 5 over 𝔽₃, 𝔽₅, 𝔽₇).
- Non-semisimple single defects are not classical either: two pairs over 𝔽₃
  with the same Gram matrix of (1, x, y) give unipotent defects with
  rank(Δ − I) = 4 and 2 (paper Remark 3.25), because for degenerate Q the
  Gram data does not decide whether xy ∈ span{1, x, y}.
- `DefectJordanType` and `DefectClassGramAndDimQ` (paper Theorem 3.29,
  Corollary 3.30; claim KILL-11): (Δ − I)(Δ² − τΔ + I) = 0 everywhere. At
  τ = −2, Δ is semisimple iff T(x) = T(y) = B(x, y) = 0, and otherwise it has
  two Jordan blocks J₂(−1). The unipotent types are 1⁸, 2²1⁴, 3²1² and 3 2² 1,
  fixed by dim Q and rank B|_Q. So a single defect's similarity class is its
  Gram data plus dim Q, and dim Q is the only non-classical datum.
- Words: W(g a) = g W(a) g⁻¹ for g ∈ Aut(C) (`WordDefectEquivariance`).
  Paper Theorem 3.32 (`WordDefectG2Determined`, formerly a conjecture)
  proves that the characteristic polynomial is a function of the Gram data,
  φ and ψ of the letters, by the first fundamental theorem for G₂
  (Schwarz; Zubkov–Shestakov in odd characteristic; literature note
  `docs/literature/g2-invariants-2026-10-05.md`). `WordDefectG2Data`
  (`data/n2/word-g2-v1.json`) corroborates it: all 979 sampled rotations of 1⊥ that preserve the
  three-form φ(p, q, r) = B(pq, r) and the four-form ψ(p, q, r, s) =
  B([p, q, r], s) of the letters' pure parts also preserve the characteristic
  polynomial. Rotations preserving only one form usually change it. So H3's
  conditioning variables are dim Q for single defects and (φ, ψ) for words
  of length ≥ 4.

## H3 pilot (`experiments/n2/h3_pilot.py`, record `H3PilotCollisions`)

Bounded experiment over 𝔽₅ and 𝔽₇, without an integral lift: norm-1
generators with fixed seeds plus their inverses, every reduced word of length
4 evaluated as a left comb. Collisions of word values split into

- **operator collisions** (95–99 %): the products L_{a₁} ⋯ L_{a₄} coincide,
  through left-alternative and similar identities, so the word defects
  coincide too. They say nothing about H3, and any defect-stratified
  statistic must quotient them out first;
- **value-only collisions** (24–546 pairs): close to the random-target
  baseline, and sharing a word-defect class at 1.5–2× the shuffled-label
  rate. The counts are too small for a significance claim.

So the pilot neither supports nor refutes H3. The next test needs an
integral octonion shell, operator collisions removed, and enough
value-only collisions to test the enrichment. It should condition on
dim Q for single defects and on (φ, ψ) of the letters for words, rather than
on the full characteristic polynomial (Theorem 3.29, Theorem 3.32).

## H3 on an integral shell (`experiments/n2/h3_integral.py`, record `H3IntegralPilot`)

Z⁸ is a subring of the definite octonions over ℚ in this model, and over a
definite algebra single defects are classical (paper Corollary 3.33,
`DefectClassicalAnisotropic`). The pilot takes reduced length-4 words in
shell generators of norm p ∈ {3, 5} and computes exactly:

- Value collisions are almost all operator collisions. In three of five
  configurations every one is.
- Value-only collisions are sporadic. They come from rare length-3
  coincidences, e.g. (g₃g₂)g₅ = (ḡ₄ḡ₀)g₂ with different operators; this
  certificate is stored in the data file.
- Where they occur (p = 3; 195 and 477 pairs), the two words share their
  word-defect characteristic polynomial in 50 % and 22 % of pairs. Computed
  exactly over all pairs, the rate is 6.8 % and 6.2 % for pairs of different
  values with equal trace, and 2.7 % and 2.1 % for all pairs of words.

So within the sporadic value-only collisions, sharing the value goes with a
shared defect class well beyond the classical invariant of the value. Whether
φ and ψ of the letters predict this is the next test.

## Conditioned integral H3 experiment (`H3ConditionedIntegral`)

**Status: bounded experiment; H3 remains open.** Theorem 3.32 (pending review)
makes φ and ψ the complete word-defect data, so form-sharing here is G₂
bookkeeping of the letters. The [exact contract and results](../experiments/h3-conditioned-shell.md)
are implemented in `experiments/n2/h3_conditioned.py`. The committed evidence
is `data/n2/h3-conditioned-v1.json`, with a digest-bound compressed bundle
certifying every collision fibre. CI reenumerates the corpus and independently
replays the certificates.

The sample unit is a `(left-comb value, integer L-product)` class of weight
one. This goes further than deleting equal-operator pairs while retaining
alias weights. Single-defect labels are the Gram data plus rational `dim Q`;
word labels are the Gram data plus every φ and ψ coordinate. Characteristic
polynomials are not conditioning labels. First and last lexicographic word
representatives are both reported because letter labels can differ within
an operator class.

The four preselected shell samples have 4,240 raw value-only pairs but only
98 quotient pairs, none sharing a Gram stratum. The subsequent structured
norm-3 panels provide 182,512 quotient collision pairs; descriptive
form-sharing ratios range from about 1.13 to 2.04 against Gram/trace-matched
different-value pairs, with a third panel giving ratio 1 and constant labels
within matched blocks. All five exploratory panels, including zero-support
ones and a norm-one calibration corpus, are retained separately. These are
dependent pairs in structured corpora, not a significance claim or a
confirmation of H3. The earlier pilot's raw class-sharing rates must not be
read as quotient statistics.

The committed census `data/n2/defect-census-v1.json` (record `DefectCensus`,
an experimental result) covers ℓ ∈ {3, 5, 7, 11} with no violations. In it
dim Q = 3 is rare (6 of about 1,930 pairs), and dim Q = 1 appears only in the
unit tests. The open research question is `DefectStratifiedCollisions` (H3):
do collision moments of navigation words, conditioned on that class, carry
signal beyond ambient conjugacy data?
