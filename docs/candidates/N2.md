# N2 — Octonionic navigation

**Status:** candidate — open questions, no lattice or expansion claim.

Let (mathcal C) denote a precisely selected integral octonion order with
(E_8) norm lattice. Before computation, fix whether generators are real
normalized maps, integral similitudes, triality triples, or reductions modulo
a prime. These are related but not interchangeable objects.

## Questions

1. Does the generated object admit an S-arithmetic interpretation and a
   natural action related to a (D_4) building?
2. For good primes (ell), what subgroup of the relevant finite orthogonal
   or spin group is generated?
3. What are the normalized nontrivial eigenvalues of the undirected,
   multiplicity-aware Cayley graph?
4. Is any covering exponent statement provable for the compact real group?

## Mandatory falsification checks

- Verify closure in the claimed group and compute determinants/forms exactly.
- Preserve duplicate generators and unit orbits until the quotient convention
  is explicit.
- Compare generated orders against the exact orders of candidate groups,
  including center/projective factors and plus/minus orthogonal type.
- Symmetrize the generating multiset or state that the operator is directed.
- Record primes excluded by bad reduction.

## Literature lanes

Search higher-rank golden gates, Ramanujan complexes, triality and integral
octonions, octonionic modular groups, Moufang/building actions, and spectral
results for finite orthogonal groups. Record exact queries and dates in a
future prior-art ledger.
